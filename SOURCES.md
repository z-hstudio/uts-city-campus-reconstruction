# Sources and evidence boundaries

Only public links and descriptions are published here; reference originals stay outside the repository and releases.

| Source | Use | Redistribution treatment |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) | Footprints, building parts, roads, walks, greens, mapped trees; snapshot 2026-09-16 | Source and derived data supplied under ODbL 1.0 |
| [UTS buildings directory](https://www.uts.edu.au/about/locations-facilities/campus/buildings) | Campus identity, addresses, major exterior form | REFERENCE_ONLY; no source photos copied |
| [UTS campus walking tour](https://www.uts.edu.au/about/locations-facilities/campus/uts-campus-walking-tour) | Landmark identity and broad route relationships | REFERENCE_ONLY |
| [UTS map](https://maps.uts.edu.au/overview.cfm) | Identity and campus locations | REFERENCE_ONLY |

Later improvements also used official architect/contractor exterior references (FJC, DCM, H2O, Hutchinson), installed shading references, and publicly visible street imagery. These were visually interpreted into authored geometry, not redistributed as source imagery, photogrammetry, texture extraction or imported CAD. Source-page availability and historical building occupancy can change. The private project retains detailed per-unit research records; private logs, links containing access credentials and original photographs are excluded.

Public dimensions are estimates unless specifically identified otherwise. No surveyed ground elevation or DEM is included.
