# Changelog

These entries map internal history to newly prepared public snapshots. They do not imply prior public releases.

## v0.1.0 — P001: Geographic foundation

Mapped building footprints/parts, roads, walks, green polygons and tree proxies established a coherent city-campus base.

Limitations: Greybox massing, floor-derived heights, flat ground; no faithful landmark facades.

## v0.2.0 — P005: First landmark identities

Tower horizontal language, Central glass volume, Engineering facade and Business School brick/glass treatments distinguish core landmarks.

Limitations: Early approximate roof and facade shapes; CB08 curvature incomplete.

## v0.3.0 — P017: Broader campus building coverage

Ordinary buildings and Blackfriars/childcare context added characteristic masonry, roofs and simple windows alongside earlier landmarks.

Limitations: Blackfriars relies partly on historical references; no certified current occupancy.

## v0.4.0 — P024-r1: Precinct landscape and connections

Alumni Green, Goods Line planting/paths, Haymarket links and Tower/Central entry proxies; revised P024-r1 includes an elevated Harris Street bridge proxy.

Limitations: Flat terrain, estimated bridge dimensions and entrances; accessibility not certified.

## v0.5.0 — P069: Current Community Edition

Later landmark corrections, mapped trunk-road completion, planting revisions, Goods Line steps/landscape/paving and final P069 mapped lamp proxies.

Limitations: Still PARTIAL; unresolved local roof/entrance/grade details and approximate surrounding context.
