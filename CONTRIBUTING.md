# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome for factual errors, source suggestions, improvements and reproducible defects. Identify the release and affected building or object; separate evidence from visual estimates. Link legitimate public references rather than uploading copyrighted photos, drawings or personal information without permission.

Only submit material you may lawfully contribute. Document original authorship and third-party licences. Contributions to the original artistic/model layer must be offered under the community licence, or clearly separated on compatible terms agreed before acceptance. OSM-derived data stays ODbL. A contribution does not authorize relicensing anyone else's content or cancelling Manyousang Z / z-hstudio as the original source of retained model contributions.

Contributors retain authorship of their additions and may be credited. No copyright assignment or commercial sublicensing of contributor-owned work is implied merely by sending a PR; any commercial rights beyond the contributor's grant need a separate written agreement. Do not include secrets, personal local paths, scans, reference photographs or unrelated campuses. Do not use issue comments as private contract storage.
