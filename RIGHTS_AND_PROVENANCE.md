## Evidence chain

Earliest retained accepted model: **P001**, saved **16 September 2026, 06:36:54 UTC**. This is a file/report evidence date, not proof of sole authorship or an independently trusted timestamp. Source OSM extraction predates it on the same day. No earlier project-start claim is established here.

The creator retains the private master files, their internally recorded SHA-256 hashes, original checkpoints and detailed modelling evidence. Private masters are **not uploaded**. A hash establishes identity of bytes, not authorship or the date those bytes were first created. Public GitHub timestamps establish this publication event, not the historical creation of geometry.

[HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md) identifies five real major snapshots. Their cleaned derivatives carry stable release UUIDs in Blender Scene properties, the `Z_HSTUDIO_ATTRIBUTION` text datablock and GLB metadata. Each release has its own SHA256SUMS and manifest naming its parent public release, assets, content commit/tag and licence version. Exact first-public timestamps are recorded after GitHub readback in `manifests/publication-record.json`; the source-preparation date alone is not asserted as publication proof.

The evidence chain is: retained historical master → private hash record → cleaned public model and UUID → publication commit/tag → GitHub release and timestamp → asset SHA-256 → downstream derivatives retaining source credit. These records support provenance; they do not replace independent copyright evidence or license third-party rights.
