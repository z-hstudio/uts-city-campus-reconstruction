# Campus publication audit framework

This project uses a source-based audit. Australian campus projects (including UTS, USYD and UNSW) should use the same framework; this document does not assert that another project has been audited or released. Other jurisdictions, including NTU Singapore, require a separate applicable-law and source assessment.

Absence of a school-specific authorization is not by itself a default blocker for all self-authored building geometry. Equally, public visibility or the use of a photograph as reference is not a representation that every underlying right has been licensed. Record rights boundaries without inventing permissions.

1. ORIGINAL_ZHSTUDIO: independently authored procedural geometry, materials and original artistic work; distinguish it from mapped source geometry and underlying architecture.
2. REDISTRIBUTABLE_THIRD_PARTY: direct CAD/BIM/scan/3D/texture assets only when redistribution permission is evidenced; keep the actual licence.
3. PUBLIC_DATA_WITH_ATTRIBUTION: OSM/GIS/DEM and similar datasets; preserve licence, attribution, share-alike and data-access obligations.
4. REFERENCE_ONLY: photos, drawings, plans and maps consulted visually but not included; do not bundle or claim ownership.
5. RESTRICTED_OR_UNCLEAR: direct assets without sufficient redistribution evidence; withhold, replace or simplify that specific asset, recording REMOVED_FROM_PUBLIC_EDITION.

Inspect packed images, dependencies, linked libraries, import/generation provenance, metadata and actual final files. Distinguish an unknown architectural legal question from an identified restricted third-party asset. Do not grant third-party rights through a disclaimer or custom licence. Apply proportional checks and preserve the private master. This framework is an audit policy, not legal advice or a legal clearance certificate.
